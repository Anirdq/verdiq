✦ Features

Everything serious traders need

Built by traders, for traders. Every feature is designed around the actual workflow of discretionary Forex & Futures trading.

📊
Performance Dashboard
Live equity curve, win/loss breakdown, R-multiple distribution, P&L by session and instrument — all in one glance.
📝
Rich Trade Logging
Log entry/exit, stop, target, quantity, fees, screenshots (before & after), setup name, timeframe, and session in a single form.
✨
AI Coach (Gemini)
Connect your Gemini API key and get personalised analysis of your trade history, psychological patterns, and actionable improvement tips.
🧠
Psychology Tracking
Tag emotions before, during, and after each trade. Track common mistakes and rule compliance to identify psychological biases.
📅
Trade Calendar
Heatmap calendar view shows your P&L by day at a glance — instantly spot your best and worst trading days.
🔬
Insights & Analytics
Discover your most profitable setups, best day of week, maximum drawdown, and most common mistake — automatically calculated.
📥
CSV Import
Bulk import trades from any broker export. Flexible field mapping means it works with MT4, MT5, cTrader, Tradovate, and more.
🧮
Risk Calculator
Calculate position size, risk/reward ratio, and potential P&L before entering a trade — then push it straight into the trade form.
🔒
Local-First & Private
Runs entirely in your browser. Optionally sync via your own Supabase database. Your trade data never touches our servers.
